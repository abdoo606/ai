# Zehnora buyer FAQ

## Is this a trained foundation model?

No. Zehnora is an assistant/orchestration, research, retrieval, memory, security,
and interface codebase. Optional open model weights are downloaded separately
under their own terms and are not represented as seller-owned assets.

## Does it require a hosted AI API?

No. The built-in mode and optional Hugging Face model paths run locally. Web
research requires network access to public sources, but model inference does not.

## Is the source exclusive?

Not perfectly. Earlier public revisions were visible while MIT was present at
repository level. The seller proposes assigning only rights the seller owns,
subject to prior grants and a counsel-reviewed disclosure schedule.

## What has been tested?

There are 76 offline tests and 82% measured package statement coverage. CI tests
Python 3.10–3.12 and package construction. Ruff, Bandit, dependency audit, clean
wheel installation, CLI, and health endpoint checks pass.

## What remains unverified?

Independent Arabic/English model-quality benchmarks on each hardware profile,
live search reliability outside the restricted development network, a third-party
penetration test, signed native installers, production pilots, and trademark/title
opinions remain open diligence items.

## Are customers, revenue, or proprietary datasets included?

No customers, revenue, production telemetry, or proprietary datasets are
represented. Runtime knowledge databases and fetched content are excluded from
the clean bundle by default.

## What support is included?

The USD 2,500 proposal includes up to 10 remote transition hours over 14 days,
subject to the definitive agreement. Ongoing support is not implied.

## How does closing work?

After identity and diligence checks, counsel finalizes the assignment/asset
purchase agreement and schedules. The buyer verifies the closing commit and
bundle hash, funds approved escrow, receives agreed controls/assets, performs the
acceptance steps, and escrow releases funds under signed instructions.

## Can the buyer rename or extend it?

The buyer may modify assets to the extent allowed by assigned and third-party
rights. “Zehnora” is only a working mark until professional clearance and an
express transfer schedule are complete.
