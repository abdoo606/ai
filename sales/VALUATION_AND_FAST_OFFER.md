# Zehnora valuation and fast-close offer

**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Purpose:** internal pricing recommendation; not an appraisal or binding offer

## Evidence-based position

Zehnora is a compact, documented technical prototype rather than an operating
business. The current repository contains approximately 3,083 Python/HTML lines,
including about 757 test lines; 76 tests pass. Measured statement coverage is
82% across the package when tests are excluded. CI passes on Python 3.10–3.12.

Value-positive assets:

- local-only Arabic/English assistant architecture;
- autonomous source-grounded research and SQLite concept-graph memory;
- deliberate local-model orchestration and model-profile installer;
- web/CLI interfaces, packaging, security controls, tests, and documentation;
- deterministic buyer-delivery bundle and transaction room.

Material discounts:

- no verified revenue, customers, active pipeline, usage, stars, or forks;
- no included model weights and no proprietary trained model;
- no independent real-model quality benchmark or penetration test;
- no native desktop GUI or signed installers;
- external live search has not been validated in the restricted build environment;
- historical public MIT exposure materially limits an exclusivity premium;
- brand/title/AI-assisted authorship and model licenses await professional review.

## Recommended quick-sale economics

| Term | Recommendation |
|---|---:|
| Public asking price | USD 2,500 |
| Expected fast-close range | USD 2,000–2,500 |
| Internal floor | USD 1,500 net to seller |
| 14-day final fallback | USD 1,250 net, as-is, max 5 support hours |
| Included transition support at USD 2,500 | Up to 10 hours over 14 days |
| Payment | 100% through counsel-approved escrow at closing |
| Buyer costs | Buyer bears its counsel, escrow, transfer, tax, and infrastructure costs |

These are negotiation steps for one complete-asset transfer—not separate product
tiers or end-user licenses. Do not publish the internal floor. Any lower price is
likely to be consumed by legal/escrow effort and is not recommended as a serious
whole-asset transaction.

## Buyer-facing fast-close offer

> USD 2,500 for a one-time transfer of the seller's assignable rights in the
> current Zehnora source asset, repository materials listed in the definitive
> agreement, reproducible delivery bundle, and up to 10 hours of transition
> support. Third-party code/model weights and prior MIT rights are excluded or
> disclosed. Closing is subject to identity/title review, signed definitive
> documents, buyer acceptance, and escrowed payment.

## Negotiation rules

1. Never call the asset perfectly exclusive.
2. Never transfer administration before cleared escrow and signed documents.
3. Trade price concessions for fewer warranties, shorter support, and faster
   acceptance—not for concealment of known risks.
4. Keep the USD 1,500 floor confidential.
5. If a buyer requests exclusivity during diligence, make it short, written,
   revocable for missed deadlines, and counsel-approved.
6. Reprice upward only after evidence exists: independent benchmarks, pilots,
   revenue, private proprietary additions, or signed buyer interest.
