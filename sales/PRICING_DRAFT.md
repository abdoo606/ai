# Zehnora pricing hypothesis — draft

These figures are hypotheses for customer interviews, not published prices.
Taxes, consumer law, support cost, model-license obligations, and payment fees
must be reviewed before launch.

| Offer | Draft price | Intended value |
|---|---:|---|
| Personal Beta | USD 79 one-time | Local app, compact/balanced profile setup guide, beta updates for 12 months |
| Pro Beta | USD 149 one-time | Powerful-profile setup, advanced research tools, priority installation support |
| Team Self-hosted | USD 2,500/year from 5 seats | Internal deployment guide, admin/security support, scheduled updates |
| Deployment service | USD 500–2,000 once | Hardware review, installation, model verification, private-network setup |

## Validation plan

Interview at least 15 target users before publishing prices. Ask about existing
workflow, privacy constraints, hardware, expected outcomes, willingness to pay,
and support expectations. Seek 3–5 design partners before a broad launch.

## Important licensing note

The repository is currently MIT-licensed, so customers can legally redistribute
covered source under its terms. Revenue must therefore come from convenient
signed builds, onboarding, support, updates, enterprise controls, and service—not
from claiming customers are unable to obtain the source. Have counsel review any
future dual-license or proprietary-module strategy before changing terms.
