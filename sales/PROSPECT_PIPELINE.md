## Qualification scorecard

Score each reply 0–2 on: strategic fit, decision-maker access, ability to close,
acceptance of MIT disclosure, technical capacity, and 30-day urgency. Prioritize
8/12 or higher. Disqualify anyone requesting concealed license history, transfer
before escrow, credential sharing, cryptocurrency-only payment without verified
identity, or an overpayment/refund arrangement.

## Outreach order

1. Send a short non-confidential teaser to the four Priority A candidates.
2. Follow up once after five business days; do not spam.
3. Ask Priority B institutions for the correct commercialization/contact route.
4. Share the FAQ before an NDA; share source bundle only after identity, fit, and
   NDA/controlled-diligence decision.
5. Record date, contact, response, next action, and consent status in a private
   seller-controlled CRM—not this public repository.
