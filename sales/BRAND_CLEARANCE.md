# Zehnora — preliminary brand clearance record

**Status:** working name only; not legal clearance.

## Why this name

“Zehnora” is a coined word intended to suggest mind/intelligence without using
the prior fictional-assistant name. It is pronounceable in English and Arabic
(`ذهنورا`) and has no descriptive claim about model capability.

## Preliminary engineering search — 2026-10-11

An exact-name GitHub repository search for `zehnora` returned zero repositories.
This is useful collision screening only. It is **not** a trademark, company,
domain, app-store, or common-law search, and it cannot establish that the name
is free of third-party rights.

## Required clearance before accepting payment

Have qualified counsel or a trademark professional search and document:

- USPTO Trademark Search (United States)
- WIPO Global Brand Database
- EUIPO eSearch plus (European Union)
- UK IPO trademark search
- Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP), if selling in Saudi Arabia
- Corporate registries in every launch market
- Relevant domains, package registries, app stores, social handles, and general
  web/common-law use
- Similar spellings and phonetic equivalents, not only exact matches

Suggested classes to discuss with counsel include Nice Classes 9 (downloadable
software) and 42 (software/AI services); actual filing scope must be selected by
counsel based on the final product.
