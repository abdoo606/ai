# Zehnora whole-asset sale overview

## Transaction intent

The seller intends to explore **one transaction transferring the Zehnora
technology asset as a whole**, not individual consumer licenses or subscription
sales. The definitive structure may be an asset purchase, exclusive assignment,
or another counsel-approved form.

## Proposed transfer perimeter

Subject to diligence and the definitive agreement, the transaction package can
include:

- Current Zehnora source code and documentation
- Test suite, CI workflows, packaging metadata, and security controls
- Research, memory, concept-graph, tool, server, UI, and local-model orchestration
- Installer/profile definitions and immutable model-manifest process
- Brand working files and preliminary collision-search record
- Commercial, security, release, and handover documentation
- Repository administration transfer after closing, if approved
- A negotiated transition-support period

## Explicit exclusions unless separately cleared

- Third-party model weights and package copyrights
- Customer/runtime knowledge databases and fetched web content
- Seller credentials, signing keys, payment accounts, and unrelated personal data
- Historical Drosophila project materials (removed from current tree)
- Any right that the seller does not own or cannot legally assign

## Critical exclusivity disclosure

Earlier public repository revisions carried a repository-level MIT license while
Zehnora code was visible. Prior grants cannot simply be revoked. The current
revision is marked proprietary for evaluation, but a buyer must have counsel
analyze Git history, contributor title, prior clones/releases, and the practical
scope of exclusivity. The clean source bundle intentionally excludes `.git` and
historical unrelated files; it does not erase public history.

## Sale process

1. Brand/title/license counsel review
2. Buyer teaser and NDA
3. Management/technical demonstration
4. Non-binding indication of interest or LOI
5. Technical, security, IP, model-license, and commercial diligence
6. Asset Purchase Agreement plus schedules
7. Closing payment through counsel-approved escrow
8. Repository/domain/signing-material assignment and verified handover
9. Time-limited transition support

No payment should be accepted from a buyer until counsel approves the transaction
structure and definitive documents.
