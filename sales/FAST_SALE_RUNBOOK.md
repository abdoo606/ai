# Zehnora 14-day fast-sale runbook

This runbook prepares a serious low-price whole-asset sale. It does not authorize
anyone to sign, make warranties, contact prospects under a false identity, or
transfer assets without the seller's approval.

## Day 0 — seller identity and legal intake

- [ ] Record seller legal name, address, tax country, payment entity, and authority.
- [ ] Give counsel `MIT_LICENSE_IMPACT_MEMO.md`, full Git history, contributor
      metadata, platform/AI-assistance terms, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- [ ] Approve asking price USD 2,500 and confidential floor USD 1,500 net.
- [ ] Decide explicitly whether the public GitHub repository should become private.
      A visibility change is not retroactive and requires owner confirmation.

## Days 1–2 — clean evidence

- [ ] Run tests, 80% coverage gate, Ruff, Bandit, pip-audit, build, and wheel smoke.
- [ ] Generate SBOM/dependency lock or mark them pending—never imply completion.
- [ ] Rebuild the clean source archive from the exact commit and store hashes.
- [ ] Record a short demo using fixed, licensed fixtures; do not expose secrets.
- [ ] Have counsel approve teaser, NDA approach, disclosure language, and LOI terms.

## Days 3–5 — first outreach

- [ ] Contact the four Priority A candidates individually using public business
      routes and the non-confidential template.
- [ ] Ask Priority B candidates for the correct commercialization contact.
- [ ] Log activity privately; do not commit buyer names/replies or personal data.
- [ ] Share teaser + FAQ + quality report, not the source bundle.

## Days 6–9 — qualify and demonstrate

- [ ] Verify legal entity and representative before confidential disclosure.
- [ ] Score prospects using `PROSPECT_PIPELINE.md`; prioritize 8/12+.
- [ ] Hold a 20-minute demo using `DEMO_AND_QUALITY_REPORT.md`.
- [ ] Ask for a written indication covering price, scope, timing, diligence, and
      acceptance; it remains non-binding except counsel-approved provisions.

## Days 10–14 — negotiate and close or reprice

- [ ] Offer USD 2,000–2,500 for an immediate qualified close.
- [ ] Do not disclose the USD 1,500 internal floor.
- [ ] If no qualified buyer exists after genuine outreach, counsel may approve a
      time-limited USD 1,250 as-is offer with no more than 5 support hours.
- [ ] Execute definitive assignment/APA and disclosure schedules.
- [ ] Buyer funds escrow; buyer verifies bundle/commit; transfer only agreed assets.
- [ ] Obtain signed acceptance, release funds, rotate credentials, remove access,
      and preserve tax/closing evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop and consult counsel if a buyer asks to hide MIT history, demands ownership of
third-party weights/content, refuses identity verification, requests credentials
before escrow, proposes overpayment/refund, or asks for unsupported legal/security
representations.
